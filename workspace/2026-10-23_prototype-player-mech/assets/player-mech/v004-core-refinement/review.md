- The long gray chest plates retain their basic shapes and placement. Oversized slotted screw discs and X-pattern engraving were removed; small fasteners, panel seams and cooling slots now suggest fabricated armor.

The bridge is fully visible and attached beneath the central torso; it is not a detached accessory. Earlier v003 wording referred to a lower waist coupling but did not include this complete bridge; v004 corrects that omission.
