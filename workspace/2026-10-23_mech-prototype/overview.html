01 / The brief

The direction so far.

Two confirmed game-design choices. Everything else is open to exploration.

StructureConfirmed

Roguelike / roguelite

The game will use a roguelike or roguelite structure. How runs work and what persists between them are still to be decided.

Gameplay inspirationConfirmed

Armored Core

The series is the gameplay reference. Specific movement, camera, combat, and customization mechanics remain open.

An independent brief

This project starts from these two choices. Project Playground's game concept, proposed scope, content counts, art direction, and estimates have not been adopted here.
