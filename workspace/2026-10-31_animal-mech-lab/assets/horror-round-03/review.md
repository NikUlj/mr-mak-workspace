# Horror Frames — concept review

Created 7 November 2026 for the **Version 3 · Horror frames** tab in Mech Lab.

## Brief and result

Explore different horror aesthetics while keeping the head, core, arms and leg chassis visibly removable and suitable for mixing across mechs. These images use detailed hard-surface rendering; the earlier low-poly constraint does not apply to this round. The horror is expressed through machine anatomy, sealed funerary armor, uncanny proportions and insect-like construction.

## Proposed modular design language

- **Head:** short circular neck collar with a visible seam beneath an independent sensor housing.
- **Core:** enclosed pilot/drive capsule; decorative armor and local wiring stay on the core.
- **Arms:** left and right shoulder docking rings with a dark clearance gap. The arm assembly carries its own shoulder shell, upper arm, elbow, forearm and wrist.
- **Lower chassis:** waist bearing above a distinct pelvis and leg assembly. Brood explores a reverse-jointed alternative to the other biped frames.
- **Hands:** conventional compact mechanical hands on visible wrist flanges. Generated hands are indicative; they do not supersede the separate Shared Hand study or establish exact finger geometry.

The visible interfaces suggest a common parts system. Exact socket dimensions, scale, weight limits, clearances and movement remain to be designed. These illustrations are concept references, not verified engineering or extracted modeling sheets.

## Visual review

All four selected images were inspected at their native 1024 × 1536 size. The heads, enclosed cores, arms and leg assemblies are legible as separate mechanical pieces. Surfaces are detailed and varied; no low-poly constraint was imposed. Studio backgrounds keep the connection seams visible.

- **Ossuary v001:** pale ribs surround a dark central capsule. Neck and waist collars and shoulder hubs are visible. Head, hands and feet are fully in frame.
- **Pallbearer v001:** the locked coffin hatch and broad silhouette distinguish the heavy frame. Its head is separate from the core; shoulder and waist rings remain readable.
- **Marionette v001:** the blank tilted mask, enamel finish and long arms produce a distinct uncanny direction. The full figure is visible, with close but intact clearance above the head.
- **Brood v001:** the outer elbow on the image's left reaches the crop. Retained as an earlier visible gallery entry, not selected as the main presentation.
- **Brood v002:** a targeted framing edit gives the complete elbow and silhouette room. Major design, palette and stance remain visually consistent; small generated details can differ from v001.

No design is marked owner-approved. No mesh, rig, game export or engine changes were made.
