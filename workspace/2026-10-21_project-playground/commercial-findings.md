## Equal-volume sales stress tests

Each route is tested at **100 / 3,000 / 20,000 new base-game buyers in its first paid year**. These are chosen scenarios, not observed genre medians or probabilities. The downside deliberately fails to cover cash costs. Price-mix and refund assumptions also vary by stress case; development stays at the middle workload/21-hour schedule here. The interactive model varies effort separately.

## Staged release: first paid year and the unfinished obligation

Paid horror chapters use $9.99 each; one-purchase horror chapters and mech EA use $14.99 initially and $19.99 for new buyers after completion. No existing owner buys the base game again. Chapter two uptake is 50% of retained earlier chapter-one owners; after completion, 60% of new entrants choose a two-chapter bundle at 10% off. These untested assumptions can be changed. ## Sales needed to cover money and time

These thresholds deliberately require the **first paid year's sales to fund all planned costs and labor through completion plus one support year afterward**, even if some work is later. That prevents a staged release looking profitable while ignoring the unfinished commitment. Middle prices/mix/refunds, 21-hour schedule. Units are initial base buyers before refunds; eligible chapter-two purchases follow the explicit conversion rules. €10/€20/€30 are example time valuations, not the owner's chosen salary.

| Route | Cash only | Cash + €10/hour | Cash + €20/hour | Cash + €30/hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Horror — full | 423 | 5,459 | 10,546 | 15,582 |
| Horror — paid chapters | 924 | 12,122 | 23,526 | 34,827 |
| Horror — chapters included (EA) | 616 | 8,079 | 15,679 | 23,210 |
| Mech — full | 495 | 6,515 | 12,586 | 18,900 |
| Mech — Early Access | 721 | 9,676 | 18,767 | 28,572 |

There is no finite break-even if realized contribution per purchase is zero or negative. The model shows this explicitly at zero price/share or 100% refunds. Required volume is not evidence that this many buyers exist. With no launch audience or comparable disclosed sales distributions, we cannot assign reliable odds to these thresholds.

## Which assumptions could reverse the interpretation?

| One change at a time | Horror surplus / h | Mech surplus / h | Scrapline surplus / h |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Middle assumptions | €5.12 | €4.16 | €4.20 |
| Half as many buyers | €2.14 | €1.67 | €1.66 |
| List prices 25% lower | €3.63 | €2.92 | €2.93 |
| Refunds/chargebacks 15% | €4.67 | €3.78 | €3.82 |
| Asset hours 25% lower | €6.01 | €4.82 | €4.72 |
| Other tool allowance €100/month | €4.58 | €3.63 | €3.67 |
| Tool allowance €0, Blender correction | €5.76 | €4.80 | €4.73 |
| 31 h/week, same total work | €5.35 | €4.41 | €4.35 |

For a concrete reversal test, matching horror's middle return/hour at 3,000 buyers would require about **3,576 mech buyers** or **3,540 Scrapline buyers**, with each candidate's current price and work assumptions unchanged. These modest volume differences are smaller than our uncertainty about demand. They are not forecasts that any title will achieve them.

- **More sales can reverse the return-on-time ranking.** The equal-volume table is not a demand forecast. Use the break-even buyer requirements as questions for market testing, not targets guaranteed by polishing the game.
- **A measured pipeline can reverse the workload comparison.** Horror motion/contact correction and mech movement/attachment/build validation are the key tests. The current mech preference remains relevant because the cash evidence does not prove a winner.
- **The incremental's advantage is a smaller commitment and earlier information.** It is still an undefined genre idea with one provisional scope. If the loop is weak, cheap assets do not create demand. A successful larger game could earn much more in total.
- **EA and episodes buy earlier feedback/income at an operational cost.** Longer gaps, weaker conversion and support above the allowance can remove the benefit. At 21 hours, increasing chapter-two uptake cannot raise first-paid-year income when chapter two ships after that window.
- **The low effort case must be earned.** These estimates are not measured evidence that the larger games need exactly three or four years. A 40-hour production diagnostic should validate or replace the decisive rates before a long commitment.
