Can the first paid year fund the whole commitment?

Buyer thresholds cover all work through completion plus one year of support afterward. They include money and labor still owed after the first paid year. Existing EA owners never buy the base game again. A chapter sale counts separately only if chapter two is ready within the measured year.

Assumptions that matter

All prices, volumes, refund rates, regional mixes, development hours and upgrade uptake are hypotheses. The low/middle/high cases are not probabilities. Income tax, unrecoverable purchase VAT, bank/FX charges, hardware purchases and business-specific overheads are outside the default amounts. There is no publisher or contractor share in the self-published baseline. Strong sales may create more support work than the default hours.
