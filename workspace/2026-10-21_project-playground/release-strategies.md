# Early Access and episodic release evidence

**Checked 21 October 2026.** These are release-structure comparisons, not evidence that staging increases profit. The intended completed scopes remain unchanged. [Return to findings](market-findings.md).

## Update burden and completion evidence

Granvir's official updates on **2, 7 and 17 October 2026** covered enemy/holster content, reporting and movement/equipment changes: gaps of five and ten days in this observed sequence. This is evidence of continuing work, not a recommended cadence or an estimate of hours per patch. Reviewers also praise support while criticizing some underlying combat and mission design. [2 October](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2743460/announcements/detail/701029225804596639), [7 October](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2743460/announcements/detail/701029860219289608), [17 October](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2743460/announcements/detail/712289492929610737).

Echoes posted development updates on **1 August and 28 September 2026**, 58 days apart. The latter describes a second campaign still progressing and plans a beta branch to make integrating the newer build easier. This directly illustrates the extra work of maintaining a sold version while developing its successor content. It is not evidence of abandonment. [August update](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2173460/announcements/detail/676253720257758799), [September update](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2173460/announcements/detail/679636495422719716).

Phantom House has an **August 2026 performance/stability patch announcement**. Its limited traction and incomplete status do not justify labeling it abandoned. MechNest has a **May 2024 input fix** after its full release; later news promoted another game. A sparse news feed alone cannot establish work hours or whether a finished product needs more content. [Phantom House patch](https://steamcommunity.com/games/3463020/announcements/detail/704403754821091925), [MechNest input fix](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1379120/announcements/detail/4193491470662590266).

M.A.S.S. Builder's post-1.0 AMA explicitly weighs new parts and features against combat rework and available money. It supports budgeting tradeoffs and support obligations; it does not disclose its profit or prove that six years is normal for indie mechs. [14 October 2025 AMA](https://steamcommunity.com/games/956680/announcements/detail/541119096409817866).

## What the episodic example actually shows

Song of Horror's third episode arrived **13 December 2019**, the fourth **30 January 2020**, and the fifth **28 May 2020**: observed gaps of 48 and 119 days. Its final episode moved from an announced 14 May date to 28 May. Delivery was completed, but the schedule was not perfectly fixed. [Episode 3](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1096570/announcements/detail/3998565024853153465), [episode 4](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1096570/announcements/detail/1692725719965899137), [earlier finale date](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1096570/announcements/detail/1701735458154990523), [revised date](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1096570/announcements/detail/1792935884901298784).

The December announcement offered individual episodes at **$7.99**, a time-limited all-episode Founder edition at **$19.99**, and a season pass at **$21.99**; these are historical USD offers, not current pricing advice. The finale announcement confirms automatic inclusion for complete-edition owners and an episodes 2–5 bundle route. Thus, a new episode was not necessarily a new sale to every existing player. We found no measured buyer-retention series or causal evidence that repeated releases improved total earnings. [Historical purchase options](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1096570/announcements/detail/3998565024853153465), [final delivery](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1096570/announcements/detail/2203895706928623182).

For our horror game, investigate a self-contained chapter arc and a satisfying ending before deciding on paid episodes. An interconnected location may depend on revisiting earlier sectors with later tools or information; cutting it into separately sold chapters can require redesign. That is a design inference from our scope, not a reported defect in Song of Horror. Episode count and purchase architecture remain open.

## Platform implications for the plan

Valve expects an EA purchase to be worthwhile in its current state and cautions against relying on a particular sales volume to complete development. Treat EA as a release with testing, communication and support work. [Valve Early Access guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/earlyaccess).

Next Fest requires an unreleased base game and a public demo. A title may participate only once; paid EA already counts as release. A demo or Steam Playtest beforehand does not by itself make the game ineligible. For Project Playground, schedule a suitable demo/festival opportunity before paid EA if that route is chosen; do not promise a date before the workload is known. [Valve Next Fest eligibility and FAQ](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest).

## Cases to cost next

| Case | First paid milestone to estimate | Later work / revenue treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full mech | Entire bounded game with stable progression and replay variety | Postlaunch fixes and optional content still consume hours. |
| Mech EA | Coherent replayable run, useful garage, varied encounters, clear endpoint and reliable saves | Added parts/areas/bosses plus balance, save migration and support. Existing buyers are not new 1.0 base-game sales. |
| Full horror | Complete interconnected 4–6-hour campaign | Fixes, localization and optional polish; no assumed ongoing content obligation. |
| Horror episodes | Finished chapter with a comprehensible promise and payoff | Remaining authored chapters plus continuity, compatibility, QA and renewed marketing. Separately paid chapters require conversion assumptions. |
| Horror campaign EA | Meaningful current campaign portion plus included later completion | One base purchase; no extra revenue from the same owner's included updates. A candidate, not the selected route. |
| Incremental full launch | Complete finite progression and ending | Pacing/balance patches and launch support; no assumed infinite endgame. |

Measure time to first payment, time to completion and total hands-on hours separately at 21 and 31 hours/week. Compare equal 12/24-month horizons from the decision date as well as first-year revenue after first paid availability. Model low early sales and delayed updates; none of the cases assumes initial customers finance the rest. This stage found no evidence sufficient to assign a reliable EA revenue uplift or episode-retention percentage.
